# DSH-DEX split Poké Ball case

The front splits at its horizontal seam. The red upper half slides up **13.6 mm** to uncover the OLED. The white lower half slides down **19.2 mm** to uncover the buttons. The white center emblem travels with the white half. The rear tray and PCB stay fixed. The exterior keychain eye has a **4.7 mm through-hole**.

## Print shop order

Send one of each of the seven STLs at **100% scale in millimeters**. The file names specify the colors. The PCB, battery, hardware, and magnets are installed after printing. The moving pieces have separate red and white color panels that glue onto their own black carriers using printed registration pegs.

## Hardware

- Four **3 mm diameter × 1.5 mm thick** disc magnets. There is one mating pair for each moving half.
- Four **M1.6 × 5 mm** plastic thread-forming screws for the retaining frame. Use heads up to 3.0 mm in diameter.
- Two **M2 × 4 mm** screws with cylindrical heads **3.8 mm in diameter and up to 2.0 mm high** for the travel stops. Match these head dimensions because the sliders contact the heads.
- Plastic-compatible adhesive for the color panels and magnets, and a split ring or chain connector.
- The assembled DSH-DEX PCB, OLED, and protected LiPo. The modeled battery pocket allows a pack body up to **24 × 19 × 4.8 mm** with leads exiting toward the connector.

## Assembly after printing

1. Glue the red and white panels onto their respective upper and lower carriers. Fit the white center emblem onto the lower carrier. Keep the guide channels clear.
2. Dry-fit the magnets. Each carrier has one open underside pocket with a matching pocket on the stationary frame. Make sure the facing poles attract, then glue each magnet at or slightly below its pocket lip.
3. Fit the battery, verify its connector polarity, and seat the PCB in the rear tray. Match the USB-C and power switch to the cutouts. Fasten the stationary frame using the four M1.6 screws.
4. Feed the red half onto the **outer rails from the top**. Feed the white half onto the **inner rails from the bottom**. Move both halves open to expose the two stop screw bosses. Install the stop screws in the stationary frame between each carrier's two stop lugs. Check that both halves reach their open and closed stops and that the magnets line up when closed.
5. Fit the keychain through the rear eye. Use the existing side power switch. Open the red half fully to align its two optical windows for IR battles.

The CAD uses the supplied PCB STEP. Print and assemble one sample to check the battery fit, sliding feel, magnet retention, and IR range.
